GVC — General Visual Line of Sight Certificate

CAA / SORA Framework · UK

The primary drone pilot qualification in the UK for operating in the Specific category. Covers operational risk assessment, airspace, emergency procedures, and flight planning.

✔ Active & Valid
🏛️

CAA Operational Authorisation

Civil Aviation Authority · UK

Permits operations beyond standard Open category limitations, including flights over people and congested areas with appropriate mitigations in place.

✔ Active & Valid
🎓

A1 Certificate of Competency

CAA · Open Category A1

Permits flight over uninvolved people with Class C1 aircraft, demonstrating thorough knowledge of airspace regulation and operational risk in populated environments.

✔ Active & Valid
🎓

A3 Certificate of Competency

CAA · Open Category A3

Authorises operations in sparsely populated areas and at extended distances — essential for rural surveys, agricultural mapping, and large-scale site capture.
